# Warsaw 2076
A route planner for a quiet, green museum outing in Warsaw. People describe an outing, compare routes and explore places. React, TypeScript, MapLibre and FastAPI remain the stack.

The 17 October user brief authorises a frontend rebuild: Moon v1 components in light and dark themes, a paper map with route-local 3D, mood-coloured routes, anchored cards, historical comparison, local demo feedback and sample promotions, and a simulated AR preview. Actual environmental scores remain proxies. Real data and simulations always retain their labels.

Explicit destinations and ordered stops depend on backend issues #84 and #85. Do not claim those constraints were applied until the API confirms them. No personal data is stored on the server. Feedback stays in this browser with export/delete controls. No real advertising or camera access.

Feature freeze: 18 October 2026, 06:00 Europe/Warsaw. Review: Ola, with Alban for map and shared-contract integration.
