# Moon over a paper city
Mode: Operate. Use Moon Design System v1, referencing https://www.figma.com/community/file/1002945721703152933/moon-design-system-v1. Direct Figma inspection was unavailable; base tokens are the team's PR #82 extraction. `design/tokens/moon.css` is the shared token source; dark mappings and mood accents are project extensions.

A calm, map-first workspace. A single left panel holds conversation, itinerary and results. A restrained header carries appearance, accessibility and sources. The map supplies the visual interest: flat muted ground with building detail around the selected route. Numbered place cards connect to real locations. Mint, lavender, amber and blue identify named moods, never measurements.

Moon surfaces, 8/16/24 radii, black primary controls, system fallback for licensed Averta. Lucide icons, distributed locally with their licence. No font service. Text has AA contrast, 44px mobile targets, visible keyboard focus, non-map access to every stop, reduced-motion support and high-contrast settings. Primary content remains usable without WebGL or online tiles.

Historical imagery uses city WMS attribution and dates. AR is explicitly a concept over a map, not a camera or navigation system. Promotions are fictional examples. Feedback records are browser-local and exportable.
